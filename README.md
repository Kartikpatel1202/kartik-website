## ✨ Highlights

- **Scroll-scrubbed hero:** a pinned stage moves through three roles (Software Engineer, Full-Stack Developer, AI Systems Engineer) as you scroll.
- **Particle portrait:** the About photo is built from thousands of particles that assemble on scroll and scatter away from the cursor.
- **3D project ring:** featured projects sit on an auto-rotating 3D carousel, each card with its own animated artwork. It supports drag, swipe, keyboard, dots and pause.
- **Case study pages:** in-depth write-ups with architecture diagrams, code excerpts and honest "known limits".
- **Work history timeline:** a scroll-drawn timeline with company logos, staggered reveals and hover effects.
- **Live-dispatch contact form:** a real-time `payload.json` preview while you type, with direct email delivery and full validation.
- **Education & certifications:** institution and issuer logos, with links to each credential.

## ♿ Accessibility & quality

- Built to **WCAG 2.2 AA**: semantic landmarks, skip link, visible focus, full keyboard support and screen-reader labels.
- **0 axe violations** on every page, desktop and mobile.
- Respects **`prefers-reduced-motion`**: every animation has a calm, static fallback.
- Static export with pre-optimised WebP images, subset fonts and lazy-loaded motion libraries.
- Strict TypeScript, ESLint and Prettier throughout.
